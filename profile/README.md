## What SkuSeal is

SkuSeal is a two-sided network for the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847). Manufacturers of products with digital elements publish their evidence once. Every importer, distributor and reseller that stocks those products then verifies and retains that evidence for their own duties, without chasing PDFs and spreadsheets supplier by supplier.

| Role                       | What they do on SkuSeal                                                                                                    |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Manufacturers              | Register products, upload evidence once, control who sees it, publish notices and answer evidence requests from one inbox |
| Importers and distributors | Import a catalogue, match each SKU to a published product, see a status per SKU, request missing evidence, build audit packs |
| Resellers                  | Receive products and notices from the distributors that invite them, with a public passport and an embeddable badge       |

## How it works

1. **Publish.** A manufacturer records each product and its evidence artefacts once.
2. **Match.** A distributor or importer imports its catalogue (CSV, XLSX or an ERP and commerce connector) and each SKU is matched to a published product.
3. **Check.** Each SKU resolves to one status against the checklist for that organisation's role: Notice open, Support ended, Missing, Partial, Expired or Ready.
4. **Retain.** Audit packs capture the evidence as it stood, with a retention record for every item.

All data is hosted in the EU.

## What SkuSeal does not do

SkuSeal records whether artefacts are present, in date and consistent. It does not determine legal compliance, perform conformity assessment, generate SBOMs, scan products for vulnerabilities or give legal advice. No certification, accreditation or compliance claim is made or implied.
